About Us

We back operators, not just ideas.

DB Holdings is a private holding company that acquires and grows well-run, founder-led businesses across essential, everyday industries.

We're not a fund looking for a quick exit. We buy in for the long haul, invest in the people already running the business, and stay close enough to help with capital, systems, and strategy — without micromanaging the trade.

Our portfolio today spans laundry services, custom home building, and mortgage brokerage: three different trades united by the same idea, that good businesses are built one satisfied customer at a time.
